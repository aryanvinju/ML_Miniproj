MACHINE LEARNING MINI PROJECT

Predicting quality-of-life decline after chemotherapy

Explore a model trained on a real public breast cancer cohort. Its target is a decrease of at least 10 points in EORTC QLQ-C30 global health status from before chemotherapy to treatment completion.

About the data

217 patients had both questionnaires. This is a small, single-study sample; the estimate is exploratory.

This logistic model was trained on 173 people and tested on 44. Its held-out AUROC was 0.7422 (bootstrap 95% interval 0.5738–0.8802). The number is not a validated individual risk estimate.

What changes the output?

Adjust one baseline score, then update the prediction. The model combines standardized scores and surgery type, then applies a logistic function.
