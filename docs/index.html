Predicting a decline in physical health during chemotherapy

Explore how pre-treatment inputs affect a model trained on fictional patients. The target is a drop of at least 10 points in PROMIS Global Physical Health (GPH).

Study context

Inspired by Ostberg & Peterson (2020). Their Stanford patient data is restricted; this model uses a reproducible synthetic cohort.

What changes the output?

Adjust one input, then update the prediction. The model combines standardized numeric inputs and one-hot encoded categories into a weighted sum, then applies a sigmoid function.
